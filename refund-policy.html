Refund Policy

This Refund Policy explains how cancellations, refund requests, duplicate payments, unauthorized transactions, and membership access issues are handled by HOMIES Trading.

Effective Date: 28 September 2026 Last Updated: 28 September 2026
Please read this policy before purchasing. By purchasing a HOMIES Trading membership, you acknowledge that you have reviewed this Refund Policy and understand how cancellations and refund requests are handled.

1. General Refund Policy

HOMIES Trading provides subscription-based membership services that may include access to private Discord channels, trading content, market notifications, live classes, educational materials, Q&A sessions, archives, and other member-only benefits.

Because membership access and digital services may become available immediately after successful payment, membership payments are generally non-refundable once processed, except where a refund is approved under this policy or required by applicable law.

This policy applies to both monthly and yearly membership subscriptions.

2. Monthly Memberships

Monthly membership payments are generally non-refundable once the payment has been successfully processed.

Cancelling a monthly subscription normally prevents the next renewal but does not automatically refund the current billing period.

You may continue to have access to eligible membership benefits until the end of the already-paid billing period, subject to the applicable membership terms and any suspension or termination provisions.

3. Yearly Memberships

Yearly membership payments are generally non-refundable once the payment has been successfully processed.

Cancelling a yearly subscription normally prevents future renewal but does not automatically refund the unused portion of the current yearly billing period.

Any exception to this general rule will be considered according to this Refund Policy and applicable law.

4. Cancellation Does Not Automatically Mean a Refund

Cancelling your membership and requesting a refund are separate actions.

Cancellation generally stops future recurring charges. It does not by itself create an entitlement to a refund for a payment that has already been processed.

Members should cancel their subscriptions before the next renewal date if they do not wish to continue the membership.

5. Duplicate Payments

If you believe that you were charged more than once for the same membership transaction due to a duplicate payment, contact HOMIES Trading as soon as possible.

After reviewing the relevant transaction information, an eligible duplicate charge may be refunded to the original payment method.

You may be asked to provide transaction details or other information reasonably necessary to verify the duplicate payment.

6. Unauthorized Transactions

If you believe a membership payment was made without your authorization, contact HOMIES Trading as soon as possible.

We may review the transaction and request information reasonably necessary to determine whether the transaction appears to have been unauthorized.

Where an unauthorized transaction is verified and a refund is appropriate, the refund will generally be issued to the original payment method, subject to the payment provider's procedures and applicable law.

If you believe your payment method has been compromised, you should also contact your bank or payment provider.

7. Technical Access Issues

If you have successfully paid for a membership but cannot access the membership services because of a technical issue, contact HOMIES Trading so the issue can be investigated.

Our first approach will generally be to resolve or restore the membership access where reasonably possible.

If a verified technical access issue cannot reasonably be resolved and the paid membership service cannot be provided, a refund may be considered.

Refund consideration may depend on the nature and duration of the issue, the services already provided, the applicable payment provider, and applicable law.

9. Review of Refund Requests

Each refund request may be reviewed based on the circumstances of the transaction and the applicable provisions of this policy.

A refund request does not automatically guarantee a refund.

We may request additional information when reasonably necessary to verify a transaction or understand the circumstances of the request.

Nothing in this policy is intended to exclude or restrict any rights or remedies that cannot lawfully be excluded or restricted under applicable law.

10. Approved Refunds

Where a refund is approved, it will generally be issued to the original payment method used for the transaction.

HOMIES Trading may not be able to redirect an approved refund to an unrelated payment method or third-party account.

The time required for a refund to appear in your account may depend on the payment processor, bank, card issuer, or other financial institution.

11. Payment Provider Processing

Membership payments may be processed through third-party payment and subscription providers.

Payment providers may have their own processing times, procedures, verification requirements, and transaction policies.

Once HOMIES Trading has initiated an approved refund, the remaining processing time may be outside our direct control.

12. Membership Suspension or Termination

If a membership is suspended or terminated because of a violation of the HOMIES Trading Terms & Conditions, community rules, unauthorized sharing of member-only content, fraud, abuse, or other prohibited conduct, the member will generally not be entitled to a refund for the remaining membership period.

This does not affect any rights that may apply under mandatory applicable law.

13. Change of Mind

Refunds are generally not provided solely because a member changes their mind after purchasing a membership, does not use the available benefits, or decides that the membership is no longer suitable for their personal needs.

Members are encouraged to review the applicable membership plan, pricing, benefits, and Terms & Conditions before completing a purchase.

14. Membership Benefits and Availability

Membership benefits may vary by plan and may be updated, modified, rescheduled, replaced, or discontinued as described in the applicable Terms & Conditions.

A temporary change or interruption to an individual feature does not automatically create an entitlement to a refund, particularly where the overall membership service remains available.

15. Trading-Related Outcomes

Refunds are not provided based solely on trading performance, trading losses, missed opportunities, market movements, or disagreement with a trade idea, market analysis, educational material, or trading outcome.

HOMIES Trading does not guarantee profits, returns, or any particular financial result from the use of its services.

16. Third-Party Platform Issues

HOMIES Trading may depend on third-party platforms such as Discord, payment providers, hosting services, and membership management platforms.

Temporary outages, account restrictions, platform changes, or technical problems originating from a third-party service do not automatically create an entitlement to a refund.

Where a verified third-party issue prevents a member from receiving the purchased service for a material period, HOMIES Trading may review the circumstances and determine an appropriate resolution, including restoration of access or, where appropriate, a refund.

17. No Double Recovery

A member should not seek or receive duplicate compensation for the same transaction through multiple channels.

If a payment dispute, chargeback, bank reversal, or payment provider claim has already been initiated for a transaction, HOMIES Trading may coordinate the matter through the applicable payment process rather than issuing a separate duplicate refund.

18. Chargebacks and Payment Disputes

Members are encouraged to contact HOMIES Trading first regarding legitimate billing or access issues so that the matter can be reviewed and resolved where possible.

Nothing in this section is intended to prevent a member from exercising rights available under applicable law or through their payment provider.
